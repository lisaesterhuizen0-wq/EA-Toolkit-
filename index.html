Meeting Brief

Pre-meeting brief, in seconds.

Paste the calendar context and the LinkedIn profile of who you're meeting. Get a structured brief — who they are, what to talk about, what to ask, what to watch for.

Paste the invite, an email thread, or a few sentences about why this meeting is happening.

Paste their LinkedIn URL, or copy the relevant 'About / Experience' text from their profile.

Your inputs are sent to Anthropic's API to generate the brief. Nothing is stored or logged.
